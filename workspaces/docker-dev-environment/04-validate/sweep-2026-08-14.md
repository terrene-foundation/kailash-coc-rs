### D1 — PR #150: the disposition is now a security call, not a bookkeeping one

Four confirmed regressions, three verified by hand and one recovered from the adjudication reports:

1. Both VCS adapters lose the own-repo `completeUpflowPR` fence (0 derivation symbols against a firing control).
2. `upstream-issue-hygiene.md` MUST-4 — the clause DEFINING that fence — is deleted outright, along with its wiring and two MUST-NOT bullets.
3. The sync-subject pattern degrades so `chore(coc-sync):` no longer matches — Gate A would over-flag every future sync (17 real sync commits missed).
4. The `reasonText`/`reasonOperand` sanitization sweep reverts to `JSON.stringify`, leaving `0x7f`, the C1 range (incl. CSI), `U+2028/9` and bidi controls verbatim in refusal-path error text.

**(a) Hold #150 until loom re-issues without the reversions.** _Pro:_ nothing is lost; the fence stays. _Con:_ the delivery this repo waited a cycle for keeps waiting, and loom may not re-issue unprompted.
**(b) Merge with per-file `ACK-DISCARD:` for the genuinely-canonical returns, re-applying the four regressions on top.** _Pro:_ the sync lands; the fence is preserved. _Con:_ a local re-apply of a synced artifact is Class-A non-durable — the NEXT sync reverts it a third time, which is exactly what the manifest entry predicted and what happened twice already.
**(c) Merge as-is and file the regressions.** _Pro:_ unblocks the queue. _Con:_ ships a known security regression on the promise of a later fix. **Not recommended.**

**Recommendation: (a), with the four regressions filed as a single upstream proposal entry naming them together.** The durable surface is loom's canonical copy; a local re-apply buys one cycle and loses the next. **Con of the recommendation, stated plainly:** it leaves the delivery unlanded for at least another cycle, and if loom does not re-issue, (b) becomes the fallback with its known third-reversion cost.
