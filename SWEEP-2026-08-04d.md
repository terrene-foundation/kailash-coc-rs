## 5. Decision points

### D1 — PR #95: hold, or merge-and-re-apply? (the only blocking one)

Merging as-is reverts a security fix that landed today. Both options restore it; they
differ in what they cost and what they risk.

- **Option A — HOLD #95 until loom ingests proposals 71–72, then re-sync.**
  Pro: the incoming tree then _carries_ the fixes; nothing is lost and nothing is
  re-applied by hand. Con (real, not hedged): the rest of #95 is 100 files of legitimate
  loom work — new audit fixtures, `_lib/repo-class.mjs`, burn-down reporting — and all of
  it waits on a Gate-1 cycle this repo does not control.
- **Option B — merge #95, then immediately re-apply `f154548`** to the two files.
  Pro: the other 98 files land now; the security fix is restored within one commit.
  Con: re-applying by hand is the Class-A treadmill, and it must be repeated on _every_
  sync until Gate-1 lands 71–72.

**Recommendation: A.** The 98 other files are not urgent — none of them is a
consumer-facing defect — while the two reverted files are the only shipped security fix
in the repo. B trades a real security window for convenience, and its re-apply step is
exactly the thing that gets forgotten on the third repetition. A also keeps the honest
signal intact: the fix's Class-A fragility is _why_ 71–72 exist, and merging over it
quietly converts a structural problem into a recurring chore.

### D2 — S-4: triage the 10 red fixture runners now, or defer?

- **INVEST-NOW.** Pro: they are the enforcement layer every fixture claim rests on, and a
  permanently-red suite reads exactly like a suite nobody runs. Con: 1–2 cycles, and the
  root cause may be loom-side, in which case this repo can only file.
- **Defer.** Pro: zero consumer-facing impact today. Con: the red is already old enough
  that nobody flagged it, which is the decay signature.

**Recommendation: INVEST-NOW, scoped to triage only** — classify the 10 into
loom-side vs local, file the loom-side ones, fix any local one. Do not commit to fixing
all 10 before knowing which are which.
